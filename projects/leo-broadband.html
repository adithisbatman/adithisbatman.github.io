LEO Broadband Market Prioritization

A percentile-based Suitability Score for prioritizing geographic markets based on demand, broadband need, and competition.

01 / The Question

Which geographic markets are best suited for an initial satellite broadband launch?

The model balances market size and expressed interest with broadband availability and competitive alternatives.

02 / Methodology

StageMethodLearning
Initial filteringMean and median thresholds across demand and broadband metricsHard cutoffs excluded promising markets.
Suitability ScoreWeighted percentile rankingContinuous rankings capture trade-offs.
DiagnosticRegression of leads on populationChecks whether interest exceeds population-based expectations.

The score represents a market hypothesis, not a validated conversion prediction.

03 / Suitability Score

Inputs were converted to percentile ranks. Competition metrics were inverted so greater competition reduces suitability.

04 / Validation & Limitations

CheckInterpretationLimitation
Positive lead residualsSelected markets generated more leads than population alone predicts.Does not establish paid conversion.
Percentile normalizationReduces the influence of skewed raw magnitudes.Depends on the comparison set.
Weighted scoringEncodes an explicit market hypothesis.Weights need sensitivity testing.
